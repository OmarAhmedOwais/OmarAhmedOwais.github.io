Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Browsing this site

This portfolio is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information to serve the site. You do not need an account to browse it.

Optional Google Analytics

Analytics stays off until a valid Measurement ID is configured and you explicitly accept. If enabled and accepted, Google processes visits, device and browser information, approximate location, and interaction events such as resume downloads and project clicks. Cookies may distinguish returning visits. Advertising features are not intentionally enabled.

Analytics does not tell me your name, email, or LinkedIn profile, and does not let me contact you automatically. Visitor-provided text and URL query strings are not sent as custom event parameters.

Change your choice through Analytics preferences in the footer when analytics is configured. Declining or withdrawing consent disables collection and clears accessible analytics cookies.

Contact and external services

Email links open your chosen email application. Copying, sharing, or downloading my contact card does not send me a message. External services have their own privacy policies. Language and theme preferences are saved locally in your browser so they are remembered on your next visit.
