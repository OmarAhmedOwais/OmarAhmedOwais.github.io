Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Browsing this site

This portfolio is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information to serve the site. The portfolio does not ask you to create an account or submit a form.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is disabled until the owner configures a valid Measurement ID and you explicitly accept analytics. When enabled and accepted, Google processes visit and interaction information, including page views, device and browser information, approximate location, and clicks such as resume downloads, project previews, and contact links. It may use cookies to distinguish visits, including returning visits. No advertising features are intentionally enabled.

Analytics does not tell me your name, email address, or LinkedIn profile. I do not send email addresses, form content, query strings, or visitor-provided text as analytics event parameters. It does not let me contact you automatically.

Your analytics preference is stored in your browser. You can change it using “Analytics preferences” in the portfolio footer when analytics is configured. Rejecting or withdrawing consent disables collection by the site and clears accessible GA cookies. You may also clear browser storage. Analytics does not load at all while unconfigured.

Contact and external services

Email opens your chosen email application. Copying an email address or sharing the portfolio does not send me a message. External links, including Google Analytics, GitHub, LinkedIn, and project websites, follow their own privacy practices. Saving my contact card stores it on your device only.
