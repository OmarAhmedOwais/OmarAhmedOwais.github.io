Inside the engineering work+

Architecture with clear boundaries

Contributed across 58 NestJS modules using CQRS, hexagonal architecture and DDD, with GraphQL and REST interfaces. Removed 41 circular module dependencies; dependency-cruiser and commit/push checks keep them at zero.

Faster paths to the data

Used pg_stat_statements to locate bottlenecks, then addressed unindexed scans and N+1 queries with targeted indexes, DataLoader batching and tag-based Redis caching. Top queries fell from 6–15s to sub-millisecond; measured cache hits reached 99.9%.

Recovery where it matters

Restored enrollment and wallet flows from 0% to 100% success in load tests. Integrated Kashier and Paymob with circuit breakers, alongside wallet and installment workflows.

Reliability backed by tests

3,000+ tests across 428 Jest/Testcontainers suites, plus 23 k6 scenarios using 1,000,000 seeded users. Coverage includes a 20→200 RPS spike and a 45-minute soak at 15 RPS, with Sentry, Prometheus and Loki for observability.
